FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about porting, provisioning, carrier features, and integrating with your Voice AI agent.

Do I need to rebuild my Voice AI agent?

No. The objective is to connect a Spenza number to your existing agent while retaining all prompts, tools, workflows, and business logic.

Can I keep using Twilio for some agents?

Yes. Start with one Spenza-powered trial while continuing to use your existing provider for other agents.

Can the same number handle calls and SMS?

Yes, subject to carrier capabilities, number type, and messaging registration requirements.

Is SMS available immediately?

SMS availability depends on registration, use case approval, and carrier requirements.

Can I port an existing number?

Eligible numbers can be ported after technical and carrier validation.

Does Spenza prevent spam labeling?

No provider can guarantee calls will never be labeled. Registration and good practices can significantly reduce risk.

How quickly can we launch?

Timelines depend on number availability, SIP configuration, messaging approval, and call-flow complexity.

Does Spenza support inbound and outbound calls?

Yes, supported integrations can be configured for both inbound and outbound calling.

Can calls transfer to a human agent?

Yes. Transfer behavior can be configured as part of the agent's call workflow.

Is this an official Voice AI integration?

Spenza connects through supported Voice AI telephony workflows. Product names belong to their respective owners.
