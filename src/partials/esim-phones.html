eSIM Phones

Stay connected with flexible eSIM technology.

Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max, Apple iPhone 16, Apple iPhone 16e, Apple iPad (2025)

In 2022, Apple launched the iPhone 14 series and the models sold in the US lacked a physical SIM slot – they relied on eSIM exclusively. This has been the case for subsequent models too.Top models with eSIMs include Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max, Apple iPhone 16, Apple iPhone 16e, Apple iPad (2025), and new iPhone models are all expected to be eSIM-compatible on a move forward basis (across most regions).

Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra, Samsung Galaxy S25, Samsung Galaxy A56, Samsung Galaxy Z Flip6

The top models featuring an eSIM for Samsung are the flagship Galaxy S models and Z foldables, but also the A-series mid-rangers. Some specialty devices like the XCover phones also have eSIM support, which simplifies using company-owned subscriptions.

Motorola razr+ 2024, Motorola ThinkPhone 25, Motorola Edge 60 Fusion, Motorola Moto G (2025)

Motorola is an interesting case – the 2019 revival of the Razr was an eSIM-only phone and since then the company has used the technology on anything from its foldables to the cheap Moto G, these are models costing $200 or less. In other words, if you want a phone with an eSIM, you can get one at practically any price point.

Xiaomi 15 Ultra, Redmi Note 14 Pro+ 5G, Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G, Poco X7

While the technology is not particularly popular in China, Chinese brands do add eSIM to their models sold internationally – e.g. Xiaomi has it on devices ranging from the Xiaomi 15 Ultra to more affordable models like the T-series and the Redmi Notes.

Google Pixel 9 Pro XL, Google Pixel 9 Pro Fold, Google Pixel 9, Google Pixel 9a

The first phone with an eSIM was the Pixel 2 in 2017. This launched on the search giant's own carrier, Google Fi. Since then, every Pixel phone from the cheaper "a" models to the Pro flagships has supported eSIM

iPhone 12, 12 mini, 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max

iPhone 12, 12 mini, 12 Pro, 12 Pro Max released in 2020 support Dual SIM-Dual Standby mode. This allows the phones to support two active lines - one on the physical SIM and other on the eSIM - allowing you to have two numbers or a domestic and international plan.

iPhone 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro Max

iPhone 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro Max released in 2019 support Dual SIM-Dual Standby mode. This allows the phones to support two active lines - one on the physical SIM and other on the eSIM.

iPhone XR, XS, XS Max

iPhone XR, XS, XS Max released in 2018 were the first phones from Apple to support eSIM. These phones support Dual SIM-Dual Standby mode. This allows the phones to support two active lines - one on the physical SIM and other on the eSIM.

iPhone SE (2020)

iPhone SE released in 2020 is the first SE edition phone to support eSIM. iPhone SE supports Dual SIM-Dual Standby mode. This allows the phones to support two active lines - one on the physical SIM and other on the eSIM.

Samsung Galaxy Fold 2

Samsung Galaxy Fold is the flagship model from Samsung to support eSIM.

Samsung Galaxy Note 20 Ultra

Samsung Galaxy Note series from 2020 sports a eSIM with Dual SIM - Dual standby support.

Samsung Galaxy S21, S21+, S21 Ultra

Samsung Galaxy S21 series launched in 2021 is the latest flagship phones from Samsung to support eSIM.

Samsung Galaxy S20, S20+, S20 Ultra

Samsung Galaxy S20 series launched in 2020 were the first galaxy series phones to support eSIM.

Google Pixel 5, 4A, 4XL

Google Pixel phones Pixel 5 , 4A and 4XL support eSIM. These phones were launched in 2020.

Google Pixel 3A, 3XL

Google Pixel 3a and 3XL from 2019 were the first Pixel phones from Google to support eSIM.
