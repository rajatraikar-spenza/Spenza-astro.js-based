Our Journey

From Travel eSIM to Agentic MVNE

We started with travel eSIM in 2020 and added a layer every year: local plans, enterprise, IoT, billing scale. In 2026, agents run the stack.

  1. 2020

    Founded Spenza

    Incorporated as a travel eSIM platform for the global traveler market.

  2. 2021

    Local Service

    Added local U.S. plans from T-Mobile, AT&T, and Verizon to the catalog.

  3. 2022

    Enterprise Plans

    Integrated directly with multiple MNOs and MVNOs to launch business connectivity plans.

  4. 2023

    IoT Enablement

    Connected Soracom, Aeris, and KORE to power the IoT MVNO use case.

  5. 2025

    Scale and Billing

    Shipped reseller support with multi-level account hierarchies and automated invoice management.

  6. 2026

    Agentic MVNE

    The operator-neutral, agent-run platform that now automates mobile operations end to end.
