    Confirm eSIM Support in 30 Seconds

    Cannot find your exact model above? These three checks work on any phone, no matter the brand or where you bought it.

    01

    The Universal Check

    Open your phone dialer and enter *#06#. If an EID number appears next to your IMEI, your phone has an eSIM. This works on every iPhone and Android.

    02

    On iPhone

    Go to Settings > General > About and look for an Available SIM or EID entry. In the US, iPhone 14 and later have no SIM tray because they run on eSIM only.

    03

    On Android

    Open Settings > Connections > SIM manager, or Network & internet > SIMs. If you can Add eSIM, your phone is ready. Menu names vary slightly by brand.

    What is an eSIM?

    An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM built into compatible devices. Instead of inserting a physical SIM card, users download a mobile profile over the air by scanning a QR code or using an activation link.

    Can I have multiple eSIMs?

    Yes. Most supported devices can store multiple eSIM profiles and switch between them. Many devices also allow using a physical SIM together with an eSIM.
