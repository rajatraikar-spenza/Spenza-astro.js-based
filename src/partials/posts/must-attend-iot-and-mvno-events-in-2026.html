Whether you’re into IoT solutions for business, healthcare, manufacturing, or smart homes, CES is an unparalleled opportunity to connect with innovators shaping the future of connectivity.

Embedded World 2026 – Embedded Systems Conference Nuremberg

Dates: March 10-12, 2026
Location: Nuremberg, Germany
Website: embedded-world.de/en

Embedded World stands as the premier global trade fair for embedded systems and IoT hardware innovation. Focusing on the intelligent devices powering modern connected systems, the event brings together developers, engineers, and system architects from automotive and industrial sectors. At NürnbergMesse in Nuremberg, participants explore comprehensive exhibitions of embedded IoT components, M2M communication protocols, and embedded vision systems. Whether you’re an embedded systems engineer, IoT hardware developer, or product manager in safety-critical industries, embedded world provides deep technical knowledge and invaluable industry connections.

FutureNet World 2026 – Network Automation Conference London

Dates: April 21-22, 2026
Location: London, United Kingdom

FutureNet World positions itself as the definitive industry event for network automation and AI-driven telecom operations. Whether you’re a network operations director, automation strategist, MVNO executive, or technology leader implementing AI transformation, FutureNet World delivers strategic insights into the next era of intelligent network operations.

MVNO NATION USA 2026 – Mobile Virtual Network Operator Conference

Dates: April 28-29, 2026
Location: Florida, USA
Website: mvnonation.com/usa

MVNO NATION USA serves as the dedicated platform for mobile virtual network operators navigating the North American market. Addressing the unique challenges and opportunities facing MVNOs, the event unites executives, wholesale connectivity providers, and technology vendors. At the Florida venue, attendees participate in MVNO business model innovation sessions, eSIM implementation case studies, and digital-first customer acquisition workshops. The conference is notable for its practical focus on wholesale agreement negotiation tactics, IoT connectivity bundling strategies, and regulatory compliance updates specific to US MVNOs. Whether you’re an MVNO executive, mobile network operator, billing and OSS/BSS vendor, or entrepreneur entering the virtual network space, MVNO NATION USA provides essential knowledge for building successful MVNO operations.
