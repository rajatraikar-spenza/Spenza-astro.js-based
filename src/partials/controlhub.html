What is ControlHub by Spenza?

ControlHub is a telecom expense management platform consolidating billing, usage, and performance data across all carriers into one dashboard. Monitor spending by subscriber, department, and device. Detect anomalies automatically.

How does ControlHub reduce telecom costs?

Real-time visibility into spending patterns reveals overage trends. Detect problems before accumulation. Identify over-subscribed users. Right-size plans to actual usage. Most customers save $600+ annually per subscriber within six months.

Can we integrate ControlHub with our existing billing system?

Yes. Pre-built connectors for Amdocs, Intrado, and major carriers available. Custom REST API support for other systems. Integration takes three to five days. Real-time data sync included automatically.

How quickly can we see ROI?

Typically six to twelve months payback. Immediate value: visibility alone reduces overspending thirty to fifty percent. Optimization recommendations drive additional twenty to thirty percent savings. Operational efficiency gains save fifty thousand plus annually.
