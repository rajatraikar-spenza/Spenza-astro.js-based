Before we start

Clear answers. Clear boundaries.

What can we evaluate first?
Start with controlled delivery testing to a T-Mobile SIP probe. Reputation observations, lifecycle enrichment, additional carriers and recurring alerts are scoped and validated for your pilot.
Can you tell whether a number has been recycled?
Only where a source supports it. Disconnect, reassignment and inventory-release records depend on your supplier and on the data access agreed for the pilot. Where a date is not source-backed, the result is returned as unknown rather than estimated.
Does a successful test mean the number is not spam-labeled?
No. Delivery and reputation are separate findings with separate sources. A call can reach the probe while the number still displays a label to recipients, which is why the two results are never merged into a single verdict.
Do you warm up numbers or guarantee better answer rates?
No. This is an evaluation service, not a remediation or traffic-shaping service. The findings are intended to inform your own registration, rotation and remediation decisions.
Do you need access to real customer conversations?
No. Testing uses controlled calls placed to Spenza receiving probes. Live customer conversations are not intercepted, recorded or sampled.
