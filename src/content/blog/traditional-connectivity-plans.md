<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Pain Points of Traditional Connectivity Plans</strong></h4>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Complex Billing Structures : </strong><br />Most traditional connectivity plans have complicated billing structures that are hard for a business to understand their actual cost. Overage charges and hidden fees make unknown expenses that put a lot of pressure on the budgets. Moreover, these plans usually require long-term contracts, locking businesses into undesirable terms with limited flexibility. This makes it administratively cumbersome to manage many mobile operators and SIMs.</li>



<li><strong>Inefficient Management of Multiple Operators : <br /></strong>The use of multiple mobile operators, SIMs, and eSIMs makes business operations complex. It leads to inefficiencies, increased costs, and potential service disruptions due to dealing with different plans and providers. To make matters worse, dealing with different billing cycles requires a coordination of monitoring usage in multiple accounts and ensuring the compliance of various contract terms. </li>



<li><strong>Lack of Transparency : <br /></strong>The old plans for connectivity lack transparency, making it difficult for businesses to track how much they use and, therefore, spend. Without visibility into their own consumption, call minutes, and other service metrics, businesses may overpay for connectivity needs and be less able to budget for future needs. This will affect the overall operational efficiency of a firm and its soundness on the financial fronts.</li>



<li><strong>High Administrative Overhead : <br /></strong>The administrative burden of managing traditional connectivity plans can be significant. Businesses often need to dedicate resources to handle billing, support, and service management, which can divert attention from core business activities</li>



<li><strong>Service Disruptions and Downtime : <br /></strong>Significant losses may occur for a business when there are service interruptions and downtime resulting from traditional connectivity plans. Outages usually equate to lost productivity, missed opportunities, and a generally unhappy customer. Downtime for customer-facing businesses means one thing&#8211; lost sales, reputation damage and lost customer loyalty.</li>
</ol>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">So, Traditional connectivity plans often present a myriad of challenges that can hinder business operations and strain budgets. Are you too worried about complex billing structures, struggling with inefficient management of multiple operators, frustrated by a lack of transparency, concerned about service disruptions and downtime, or burdened by high administrative overhead? These issues can significantly impact your productivity and overall success.</p>
