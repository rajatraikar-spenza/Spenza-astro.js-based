<li>Built to keep gameplay smooth during tournaments and live sessions.</li>
</ul>

Offer “<strong>game-only</strong>” data packs that don’t count toward the main pool. Example: <em>10 GB free data monthly for BGMI or COD Mobile.</em> This simple change boosts loyalty and usage simultaneously.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Custom data packs for specific games.</li>



<li>Encourages more playtime without fear of data caps.</li>



<li>Increases retention by offering visible, real benefits.</li>



<li>Encourages direct monetisation of <strong>fan engagement</strong>.</li>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It also creates a clear path to <strong>monetise fan engagement</strong>. Fans see real value when you improve how they play, not just what they buy. Plans can even bundle esports subscriptions or in-game rewards. When you tie connectivity directly to gameplay, the line between telecom and entertainment disappears.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Feature 3: Community and Perks Layer</strong></h3>
