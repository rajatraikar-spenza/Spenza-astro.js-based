<h2 id="MVNO-Landscape" class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Understanding the MVNO Landscape</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MVNOs today face both exciting opportunities and challenges. The rollout of 5G is creating a lot of buzz, but MVNOs need to plan carefully to compete effectively and ensure they have good network coverage. Increased switching due to more MVNOs offering compelling plans as eSIMs gain traction means heightened competition. The exploding IoT market is also a key area of growth but requires focused new connectivity solutions. At the same time, customer experience is everything. An MVNO cannot afford to ignore service quality, tailored offerings, or even smooth digital touchpoints. The MVNO market is changing as companies merge and partner with each other. MVNOs are looking to acquire other companies to grow their reach and services.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">With every trend, however, comes a different set of challenges that impact growth and customer retention. For MVNOs, the focus should be on developing tailored offerings for specific demographics or needs to get a market foothold.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Complex Network &amp; Service Integration:</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pain Point: The MVNE&#8217;s partnership with multiple MNOs multiplies the level of effort significantly for overall service integration. Every MNO probably has their own tools and systems, which makes service provisioning, handovers, and roaming services difficult to manage for the MVNO. This complexity can result in service launches taking longer, increased spending on operations, and poor customer satisfaction. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>How Management Solutions Help:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Standardized Interfaces:</strong> Management solutions can provide set interfaces for manual operations, which would make the interconnection with multiple MNO systems easier, less costly, and not require much effort in the way of new custom-developed interfaces. This allows the MVNO to control several network connections from one platform.</li>



<li><strong>Automated Provisioning: </strong>Automated provisioning instruments resolve the issue of activating and deactivating services for subscribers in various networks, which can be done without human intervention. This helps remove manual work, decreases mistakes, and improves the speed of service provisioning.</li>



<li><strong>Intelligent Routing: </strong>Modern routing procedures allow seamless redistribution of traffic within the various MNO networks for the highest possible service quality for subscribers. This can include using the strongest signal or lowest latency for routing calls and data.</li>



<li><strong>Roaming Management: </strong>Management solutions can deal with roaming agreements’ complexities automatically, including rate control, data clearing, and billing. It enables subscribers to have uninterrupted seamless roaming services while minimizing revenue loss for the MVNO.</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. ‘Revenue Leakage’ &amp; Billing:</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pain Point: Precise and prompt billing is essential for MVNOs. Unfortunately, interfacing with multiple MNO plumbing systems and coordinating elaborate pricing configurations can be very difficult. Profitability can be affected due to inaccuracy in billing, fraudulent activities, and inefficient usage monitoring.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>How Management Solutions Help: </strong> </p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Real-time Billing Systems: </strong>Real-time billing avoids the hassles and inaccuracies often tied with batch billing by providing precise usage tracking and automated rating. This not only enables MVNOs to bill their subscribers efficiently but also eliminates the need for time-consuming manual pumps and endless billing and accounting bureaucracy.  </li>



<li><strong>Convergent Billing: </strong>The convergent billing solution enables MVNOS to combine and bundle diverse services touching voice, data, and messaging, which can be billed at the same time, thus making billing less complicated for subscribers and increasing operational efficiency.  </li>



<li><strong>Fraud Management: </strong>The Fraud detection module helps to retain revenue by identifying and preventing SIM swapping, subscription fraud, or call bypass activities.  </li>



<li><strong>Revenue Assurance: </strong>Revenue assurance systems are put in place to keep tab on the network traffic alongside the billing data to identify unbilled revenues, improper rate setup, or misplaced discounts. Thus, the MVNOs further enhance revenue streams.  </li>



<li><strong>Transparent Billing: </strong>Billing disputes can be lessened or done away with entirely by having subscribers view their real-time usage and billing information on online portals or through mobile apps. </li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. Subscriber &amp; Plan Management:</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pain Point: MVNOs have to provide personalized plans to attract and retain users truly. However, the scope of automation is wide when dealing with various plans, data bundles, promotions, and even top-ups. In the absence of sufficient resources, all of this becomes challenging to manage.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>How Management Solutions Help:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Flexible Plan Design: </strong>Management platforms allow MVNOs to set up particular pricing plans along with the data bundles and promotional offers. This means they can create plans for specific customer segments.  </li>



<li><strong>Self-Service Portals:</strong> Self-service portals enable subscribers to control their accounts and change subscriptions, purchase add-ons, and track their usage. These capabilities lessen the burden on customer services and improve customer satisfaction.  </li>



<li><strong>CRM Integration:</strong> MVNOs can provide targeted offers and messages to subscribers by integrating management platforms with CRM systems, which allows for segmentation based on subscriber preferences and usage. This can significantly improve the retention of customers and reduce churn.</li>



<li><strong>Automated Marketing:</strong> By utilizing marketing automation tools, MVNOs are capable of sending individual tailored promotions and offers to subscribers, thus ensuring their interest and helping to boost sales.</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>4. Scalability &amp; Growth Challenges:</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pain Point: As with many MVNOs, growth can come with a set of challenges, the most prominent of them being scaling operational capabilities. Entering new markets or even offering new services tends to be resource heavy and capital intensive. Trying to grow competitively while also using manual processes makes elasticity sluggish and growth financially unfeasible.  </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>How Management Solutions Help:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Cloud-Based Solutions:</strong> MVNOs can now easily manage the scaling of their operations without significantly investing in new resources with advanced cloud-based management systems. Additionally, cloud management systems provide <a href="/blog/mvno-types-a-comprehensive-guide-to-understanding-them/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">MVNOs </a>with the ability to flexibly and quickly scale their operations whenever it is needed. This allows MVNOs to support their rapid growth without investing in new resources.  </li>



<li><strong>Automation:</strong> A number of processes such as service provisioning, billing, and customer support can and should be automated. This reduces the need for human interaction, speeds up the process of operation, and helps improve the efficiency of MVNOs. As a result, MVNOs can effectively meet their growing operational needs.  </li>



<li><strong>API Integrations:</strong> MVNOs can now take advantage of open APIs integrations with payment gateways, CRM systems, and marketing automation tools to improve the efficiency of their day to day operations. This further enhances the capability of the management platform as the data is seamlessly exchanged and integrated.  </li>



<li><strong>Analytics and Reporting:</strong> With the ability to monitor subscriber habits and market changes, MVNO’s can formulate smart expansion and marketing strategies powered by their own data analytics and reporting tools.</li>
</ul>
