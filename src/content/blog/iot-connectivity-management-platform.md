<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Being tied to a single carrier can be limiting, especially as your IoT devices expand to new markets. Lack of multi-network support or global coverage could mean outages, higher costs, or connectivity blind spots.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If keeping your system running requires constant manual intervention or results in frequent outages, it’s more than a technical issue—it’s a business risk.</p>
