<h2 id="Difference" class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What Is the Difference Between BSS and OSS for MVNOs?</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">MVNOs often need both BSS (Business Support Systems) and OSS (Operations Support Systems), but they serve different functions.&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>BSS focuses on the business side. That includes handling customers, managing subscriptions, processing payments, sending invoices, and creating plans.&nbsp;</li>



<li>In contrast, OSS manages the technical part of the servicethings like network provisioning, SIM activation, device authentication, and service assurance.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Think of BSS as everything related to the customer experience and revenue.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">OSS takes care of service delivery and network functions.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For example, if a customer signs up for a mobile plan, the BSS sets up their billing profile, applies taxes, generates the invoice, and stores the customer’s plan data.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The OSS ensures that the SIM card gets activated, the device connects to the right network, and the service works without interruptions.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An MVNO without a strong OSS may face SIM activation failures or dropped connectivity. Without strong BSS, it can’t bill customers properly or manage plan upgrades.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Together, they form the backbone of MVNO operations. But not all vendors offer both. When choosing a MVNO BSS provider, ensure they work well with your OSS or provide OSS services too.</p>
