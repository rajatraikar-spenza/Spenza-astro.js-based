<p class="wp-block-paragraph">All the plans and SIMs available for purchase through Spenza are accessible in the <a href="/">Spenza Marketplace</a> under Plan Catalog and Sim Catalog respectively. When we create a profile for your business, we gather a comprehensive list of both international and domestic providers, including T-Mobile service plans and other leading options, along with their available plans. These plans are featured in our catalog, allowing you to view and order them directly via Spenza.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The marketplace ensures that all listed plans are integrated with operators, providing real-time availability for immediate purchase. Similarly, we maintain a SIM catalog, which includes all the SIMs available for purchase on the Spenza platform.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Requesting a Custom Plan</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 1 :</strong> Navigate to the Service Management → Service Requests and Select <strong>Plan Requests</strong> tab. Click on <strong>Add Request</strong>.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="427" class="wp-image-5552" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-21-1024x427.png" alt="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-21-1024x427.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-21-300x125.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-21-768x320.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-21-1536x640.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-21.png 1920w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 2 :</strong> Customize the plan details according to your needs.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="427" class="wp-image-5553" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-22-1024x427.png" alt="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-22-1024x427.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-22-300x125.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-22-768x320.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-22-1536x640.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-22.png 1920w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 3:</strong> <strong>Submit</strong> your request. You will receive a confirmation that your request has been successfully submitted with the status marked as &#8220;requested.&#8221;</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="427" class="wp-image-5551" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-20-1024x427.png" alt="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-20-1024x427.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-20-300x125.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-20-768x320.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-20-1536x640.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-20.png 1920w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Spenza admin will review your request and proceed to provide the plan. The admin can check, fulfill, and update the request&#8217;s status as changes occur.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Purchasing a SIM Card</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 1 :</strong> To purchase a SIM card from Spenza navigate to the <strong>Service Request</strong> → <strong>Sim Purchase Requests</strong> tab. Click on &#8220;<strong>Purchase SIM</strong>&#8221; button.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="427" class="wp-image-5550" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-19-1024x427.png" alt="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-19-1024x427.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-19-300x125.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-19-768x320.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-19-1536x640.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-19.png 1920w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 2 :</strong> Select the preferred SIM, including options compatible with T-Mobile service plans, and fill in other required details such as address and desired delivery date.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="426" class="wp-image-5549" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-18-1024x426.png" alt="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-18-1024x426.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-18-300x125.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-18-768x320.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-18.png 1371w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 3 :</strong> Proceed to pay using your credit balance or <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_card">credit card</a>. After the purchase, you will receive a notification by mail. The request status will reflect ‘Payment Succeeded,’ and you can view the order details.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Step 4 :</strong> Similar to a plan request, the Spenza admin can update the order status on their end as any changes occur.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="427" class="wp-image-5548" title="" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-17-1024x427.png" alt="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-17-1024x427.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-17-300x125.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-17-768x320.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-17-1536x640.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-17.png 1920w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Spenza’s integrated platform also supports businesses working with international <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payroll">payroll </a>providers, ensuring seamless communication and alignment of mobile service needs globally.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This concludes how to request a plan or purchase a SIM from the Spenza Marketplace, ensuring you optimize your mobile services efficiently.</p>
