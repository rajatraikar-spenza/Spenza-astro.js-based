<p class="wp-block-paragraph" id="viewer-7v1cd">When companies face pressure to reduce costs as margins get squeezed, one of the areas that often faces scrutiny is mobile phone service. Mobile operators are notorious for making billing and service plans complicated and continuing to charge premium rates when lower cost alternatives are available. Some companies even consider eliminating mobile service altogether due to the cost and complexity of managing the service.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" id="viewer-bn13m">The financial calculation that goes into this decision is often more nuanced than just “we pay X, let’s get rid of that cost.” Mobile services are an essential part of modern business both for customer accessibility and the flexibility to work from anywhere. Companies that have offered company-paid service in the past are loath to totally eliminate the benefit as employees will rightly see this as a pay cut and be less likely to respond outside of office hours. Businesses often arrive at the compromise solution of allowing employees to expense their personal mobile service.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph" id="viewer-9tg8s">While this may seem like the best of both worlds &#8211; employees can pick the service they want and the company doesn’t have to manage it &#8211; that approach has hidden costs that companies often overlook.
