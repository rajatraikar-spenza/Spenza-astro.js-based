<h2 id="Challenge" class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Challenge: Why Launching an MVNO is So Hard (Without the Right Partner)</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For consumer device OEMs, IoT startups, and hardware brands, the path to becoming an MVNO has traditionally been littered with obstacles that stifle innovation and drain resources.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">While your team is savvy in product design and understands connectivity dynamics, you likely lack the deep telecom stack experience required to navigate the industry’s complexities.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">These <a href="/telecom/full-mvno/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">challenges</a> often prevent brands from realizing the full potential of their connected devices.&nbsp;</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Common pain points include:</strong></h4>

<li><strong>High Capital Expenditures:</strong> The investment in engineering capex, infrastructure, and technology can be a significant and often unjustifiable barrier to entry.</li>



<li><strong>Regulatory Nightmares:</strong> Navigating the complex web of telecom and privacy regulations—like <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Know_your_customer" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">KYC</a>, <a href="https://yourcommsgroup.com/case-study/what-is-telecoms-reseller/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">reseller rights</a>, SOC-2, and <a href="https://www.taxconnex.com/en/understanding-telecom-taxes#:~:text=against%20certain%20revenue.-,Telecom%20Tax%20Obligations,way%20fees%2C%20USF%20and%20more." target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">tax obligations</a>—is a full-time job in itself.</li>



<li><strong>Business Stagnation:</strong> For many device vendors, reliance on single-operator solutions can lead to low customer satisfaction, high churn, and shrinking profit margins.
