<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Around 30% of global smartwatch shipments in 2023 included cellular connectivity, and nearly all of them used eSIM. In the U.S., every major carrier allows adding these watches to an existing plan.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">That means the hardware is already capable of activating a roaming eSIM for travel. A smartwatch runner flying from New York to Paris can activate a France plan right from their watch. Your brand could be the one selling that plan.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By 2024, more than 50% of new U.S. tablets will ship with eSIM built in.
