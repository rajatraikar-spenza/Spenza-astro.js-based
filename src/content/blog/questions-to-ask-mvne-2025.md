<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Definition, in one line</strong>: An <a href="/mvno/mvno/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">MVNO </a>(mobile virtual network operator) sells mobile service over existing MNO networks without owning the radio access network; in the US, most new MVNOs launch via an <a href="/mvno/mvne-explained/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">MVNE </a>to reduce time-to-market and upfront cost.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Top 10 Questions to Ask Your MVNE Partner</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large is-resized"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1024" height="605" src="/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/The-10-Questions-to-Ask-Your-Potential-MVNE-Partner-visual-selection-1024x605.png" alt="Questions to Ask Your MVNE" class="wp-image-11907" style="width:666px" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/The-10-Questions-to-Ask-Your-Potential-MVNE-Partner-visual-selection-1024x605.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/The-10-Questions-to-Ask-Your-Potential-MVNE-Partner-visual-selection-300x177.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/The-10-Questions-to-Ask-Your-Potential-MVNE-Partner-visual-selection-768x454.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/The-10-Questions-to-Ask-Your-Potential-MVNE-Partner-visual-selection-1536x908.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/The-10-Questions-to-Ask-Your-Potential-MVNE-Partner-visual-selection-2048x1210.png 2048w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1) Strategy First: BYON + Marketplace</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Can we <strong>bring our own carrier agreements (BYON)</strong> and still manage everything in one pane of glass?</li>



<li>If we don’t have contracts, can we <strong>go live via your multi-operator marketplace</strong> in days—not months?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why it matters</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">BYON maximizes control and cost leverage; marketplace gives you instant coverage and time-to-market. The best MVNEs support <strong>both</strong>, so you can optimize by country/device segment as you scale.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Spenza supports <a href="/spenza-product/purchasing-spenza-marketplace-plans/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Spenza Marketplace</strong></a><strong> &amp; </strong><a href="/spenza-product/unlock-operational-efficiency-with-spenza-iot-aggregation-platform/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>BYON</strong></a> to add new coverage quickly and grow with ease.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2) Network &amp; Coverage Readiness</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Which <strong>Tier-1 MNOs</strong> do you have direct deals with? How strong are those partnerships?</li>



<li>Can you show <strong>QoS/QCI</strong> transparency, VoLTE defaults, 5G SA/NSA roadmap?</li>



<li>Evidence: roaming footprint maps, sample SLAs, escalation matrix with MNOs.</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why it matters</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your MVNO’s experience equals the quality of the host networks and your MVNE’s leverage with them. Probe for <strong>direct Tier-1 ties</strong> and SLA-backed escalation paths.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3) eSIM &amp; Device Readiness</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Do you support <strong>SM-DP+/SM-DS flows</strong> and in-app activation UX?</li>



<li>Can you demonstrate <strong>activation success rate</strong> and <strong>time-to-first-packet</strong>?</li>



<li>What’s your roadmap for <strong>SGP.32</strong> (next-gen eSIM for devices)?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Reality check</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You’ll need today’s <a href="/esim/esim/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">eSIM</a> orchestration and tomorrow’s standards. Spenza orchestrates eSIM to minimize SKUs and has <a href="/esim/sgp-32-new-esim-standard/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>SGP.32</strong></a> on its roadmap.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>4) APIs, No-Code, and Integration Depth</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Is there <strong>API parity</strong> for provision/swap/suspend/plan changes, webhooks, and audit trails?</li>



<li>Is there a <strong>no-code</strong> layer for non-technical teams and <strong>Shopify</strong> integration for direct commerce?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why it matters</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">APIs drive automation; no-code accelerates launch and ops. Spenza provides a <strong>no-code MVNE platform + integrated </strong><a href="/mvno/mvno-shopify-launch/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Shopify app</strong></a> for fast execution.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Check out Spenza’s detailed API documentation:<a href="https://api.preprod.spenza.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> https://api.preprod.spenza.com/</a></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>5) Billing &amp; Monetization Architecture</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>How do you support <strong>prepaid/postpaid</strong>, add-ons, shared pools, promos?</li>



<li>Do you provide <strong>CDR rating &amp; billing</strong> (or is it on your roadmap)?</li>



<li>Can we export data to BI/finance and reconcile cleanly?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Spenza status</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>CDR rating &amp; billing</strong> is on Spenza’s near-term roadmap; the current platform supports <a href="/telecom/integrated-connectivity-management/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">unified connectivity management</a> and <a href="/spenza-product/spenza-super-admin-overview/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">integrated billing</a>.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>6) Compliance, Tax, and KYC/eKYC</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Which <strong>telecom taxes</strong> and regulatory filings do you handle vs. what do we handle?</li>



<li>Do you support <strong>KYC/eKYC</strong>, <strong>GDPR/CCPA</strong>, and <strong>lawful intercept</strong> hooks?</li>



<li>What’s your <strong>global compliance roadmap</strong>?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Spenza approach</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Spenza includes<a href="/mvno/2025-mvno-guide-telecom-regulatory-success/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> <strong>US telecom tax integrations</strong></a> and supports <strong>KYC/eKYC</strong>, with <strong>global compliance</strong> as a declared roadmap. Expect pre-integrated tax/regulatory workflows and SOC progress for trust.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Pro tip:</strong> Ask every MVNE to <strong>list, in writing</strong>, which regulatory filings they perform as your agent and what indemnities apply if they miss.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>7) Operations, SLAs, and Support Velocity</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>24/7 support? Response/Resolution SLAs? <strong>Escalation matrix to MNO</strong>?</li>



<li>Training for your staff and an <strong>agent portal</strong> that solves first-contact issues?</li>



<li>Inventory, eSIM/SIM/device tools, and <strong>analytics dashboards</strong> out of the box?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why it matters</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Weak MVNE–MNO escalation = delayed fixes and churn. Demand contractual SLAs and tooling that shortens time-to-resolution.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>8) Pricing &amp; Commercial Clarity (Avoid the Gotchas)</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Full breakdown of <strong>setup</strong>, <strong>recurring</strong>, and <strong>usage-based</strong> fees; <strong>minimums</strong> and <strong>volume tiers</strong>?</li>



<li>Any <strong>activation fees</strong>, roaming mark-ups, or penalties? <strong>Exit/migration</strong> terms?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why it matters</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Opaque pricing erodes margins. You need volume-tier clarity and flexibility—especially early on.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Spenza stance: Transparent fees</strong>, <strong>volume-based tiers</strong>, <strong>low upfront setup</strong>, and <strong>no long-term lock-ins</strong>. (Low setup and tiered model referenced in internal pricing notes.)</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Learn more about MVNO billing platforms:<a href="/mvno/mvno-billing-platforms/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"> /mvno/mvno-billing-platforms/</a>&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>9) Security &amp; Reliability</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Is the platform <strong>HA with geo-redundancy</strong>? What are <strong>RTO/RPO</strong> targets?</li>



<li>Failover for <strong>HSS/BSS/APIs</strong>? Incident communication protocols?</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why it matters</strong></h4>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You need documented architecture and DR objectives to safeguard revenue and reputation.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>10) Analytics &amp; Reporting</strong></h3>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What to ask</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Do you provide <strong>dashboards</strong> (activation success, churn, ARPU, usage) and <strong>raw exports</strong>?</li>



<li>Alerts and anomaly detection for fraud/abuse?</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Why it matters</strong><strong><br></strong>Fast insights power pricing, promos, and cost control.&nbsp;</p>
