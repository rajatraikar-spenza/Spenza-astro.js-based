## M07 body 2: Responsibility roles

- Placement: After commercial ownership, before cost breakdown.
- Files: `white-label-mvno-responsibility-roles.png` and `white-label-mvno-responsibility-roles.webp`.
- Alt text: Buyer, retail provider, platform and network roles, with responsibilities to confirm in the contract

**Stage 1: copy this prompt into ChatGPT**

```text
Create a clean, modern B2B SaaS infographic for a telecommunications platform
brand. Flat vector illustration style. Palette: warm cream background
`#FDF5F2`, white cards with soft shadows, deep ink `#1A1A2E` for headings, grey
`#4E545D` for body text, and a single accent of vivid orange `#FF4500` used
sparingly for emphasis. Geometric sans-serif typography (Montserrat or Inter).
Generous whitespace, strong alignment to an invisible grid, everything level
and symmetrical. No photorealism, no 3D renders, no drop-shadowed bevels, no
gradient meshes, no stock-photo people, no clip-art, no emoji. 1600×900
pixels, 16:9. Any text must be large, minimal and correctly spelled.

Shape: a horizontal chain of four equal white cards, with a separate wide cream note bar below. Allow 80px outer margins and a clear empty title area. This is a role map, not a legal entity chart.
Positions: left to right, reserve space for the exact labels "Buyer", "Retail provider", "Platform operator", "Network supplier". Use simple consistent line icons: person outline, storefront, server, radio tower. Under each reserve two lines for "Service terms", "Customer obligations", "Agreed functions", "Wholesale access", respectively. Thin neutral connectors join adjacent cards. In the note bar reserve space for "Confirm entities and responsibilities in the contract".
Colour: all roles have equal white cards with ink icons. Orange outlines highlight the note bar only; orange must not imply that a particular company owns a responsibility.
Exclude: company names, logos, fabricated contracts, compliance badges, price or timing claims, hierarchy arrows, tiny text, gradients and decorative objects. A company may fill multiple roles. Generate the diagram frame and icons ONLY, leaving all label areas blank. The quoted labels are the exact typography plan for a later Figma or Canva pass, not text to render in this generated frame.
```

**Stage 2:** Place the exact quoted labels in the reserved areas using Figma or Canva. Keep the icons and connector logic as specified; do not add claims, statistics or screenshots. Check spelling, contrast and mobile legibility before export.
