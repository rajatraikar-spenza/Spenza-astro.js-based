No white background, no outlined word boxes. A single large dimensional personal smartphone at center. Above it, a sculptural key and identity badge with a circular control arc labelled ACCESS. Beside its screen a translucent work briefcase chamber with file and padlock objects labelled WORK DATA, a separate small photo/heart object stays outside that chamber. Below the phone a SIM chip linked to a radio tower, labelled MOBILE SERVICE. These are THREE DISTINCT controls, not a pipeline and not automatic app routing to the SIM.
