## invoice-record-anatomy.png

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame
with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange
`#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white
background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a
soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are
confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with
layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and
objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with
blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a
single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat
outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels,
16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

An exploded evidence view of one telecom invoice, with its inputs visually attached. A large off-white paper invoice at centre-right, a mobile usage meter at upper left, a tariff rulebook with calculator at lower left; two separate broad curved ribbons from these objects join the invoice. A distinct payment receipt and coin rest beside the invoice at far right, connected with a thin slate thread, not confused with usage. Hand-painted diagrammatic objects, shadow sides, warm bright orange field. No numbers, no currency amounts, no fake UI, no text anywhere. Reserve blank bottom strip y=760..900 for separately typeset labels. Art in y=100..700. Objects make many-inputs-to-one-bill visible. No boxes or cards, no brown or burnt sienna. Final label texts added later: "Usage", "Tariff", "Invoice", "Payment".
