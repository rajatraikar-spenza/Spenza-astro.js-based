## audit-evidence-dossier

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange `#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels, 16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled. A conceptual exploded paper dossier viewed straight-on. At the center a single invoice receipt has one orange irregular highlighted line, with three physical paper tabs tethered to that same line by short bold threads: a signed contract scroll, a SIM inventory tag, and a supplier response envelope. All evidence stays attached to one finding. A magnifying lens reveals the attachment point rather than an invented number. No text or digits, no checkmark of automatic success, no rows of labeled boxes. Layered hand-drawn paper objects on the orange field, grain, glow, ink shadow sides and off-white highlights; not a 3D render.
