## tem-record-alignment

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange `#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels, 16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled. A conceptual cutaway, not a diagram. Three overlapping translucent paper objects converge on a single circular service identity token: a contract scroll with a signature mark, a long invoice receipt, and a SIM-shaped inventory tag. Their matching circular cutouts align perfectly only at the shared center, with one offset receipt edge showing a mismatch. Visible overlap and alignment convey the relationship before reading. Off-white paper, ink silhouettes, orange light spilling through the aligned holes. No arrows in boxes, no charts, no numbers, no lettering whatsoever. A bold hand-drawn editorial collage with paper grain and layered shadow sides, not photorealism or a 3D render.
