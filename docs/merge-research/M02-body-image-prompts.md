# M02 body-image prompts

These prompts use the four-stop palette. Generated with the image_gen tool; no hero or published asset is an input.

## mvno-network-and-operating-roles.png

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame
with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange
`#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white
background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a
soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are
confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with
layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and
objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with
blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a
single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat
outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels,
16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

A spatial role map, not a traffic routing diagram. Centre a substantial illustrated branded shopfront labelled "MVNO"; left a customer's mobile phone labelled "Customer"; right a radio mast labelled "MNO". A solid orange ribbon connects customer to shop (commercial service), an ink ribbon connects shop to mast (network access). Below shop, a server and cog labelled "MVNE" connects to shop by a dashed slate line for optional systems. Below the network-access ribbon, three small bundled contract sheets labelled "MVNA" connect to that ribbon with a dashed slate line for optional aggregation. Each role is a recognizable object with a lit face and shadow side, not a rectangular text card. One cohesive editorial scene with generous separation; connections must not cross. Exact text only these five role names; no small text or title. Bright #FF4500/#FF6F39 field with #C63600 deepest orange; no burnt brown, no dark vignette. Four stops only. Keep symbols diagrammatic and avoid suggesting MVNE/MVNA are mandatory traffic hops.
