# M11 image prompts

Built-in image generation; new body images only. Inventory precedes generation. Published destination hero is referenced, not copied.

## reseller-service-handoffs

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange `#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels, 16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

A wide left-to-right flowing service relationship: a bold radio tower and network cloud at left, a gear surrounding an eSIM chip in the centre, a branded blank storefront with small support-headset object at right, and a customer handset beyond the storefront. Broad curved arrows join tower to platform to storefront to handset; one return curved arrow from handset to storefront shows support. Exact labels below each object: "NETWORK", "PLATFORM", "RESELLER", "CUSTOMER". White labels sit on deep orange #C63600 patches only. The storefront is the focal object. Orange brightens toward the customer. No vendor logos, no invented screenshots, no boxes, no claims of automatic failover. Never use #9B2A00. Never set text directly on #FF4500.
