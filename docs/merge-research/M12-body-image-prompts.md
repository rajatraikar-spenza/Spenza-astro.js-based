## mandatory-fit-barrier.png

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame
with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange
`#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white
background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a
soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are

layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and
objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with
blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a
single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat
outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels,
16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

Illustrate a procurement decision where preferences cannot compensate for a missing mandatory capability. At left a weighing scale carries several small star-shaped preference tokens. At right a substantial locked gate blocks a small telecom device from proceeding. The scale's arm points toward the gate but cannot unlock it; a single key-shaped requirement slot in the gate is visibly empty. The spatial barrier, not a score, dominates. Off-white scale and device, cool blue-grey lock, ink shadow sides on a bright orange four-stop field with grain and focal glow. No text, no letters, no numbers, no vendor logo. Art only. Reserve bottom strip for later typography. No brown, beige or gold. No tables, cards or rectangular panels.

Typography stage: "Preferences", "Mandatory fit" in Figma Inter Bold.
