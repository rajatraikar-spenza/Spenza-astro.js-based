# Body-image rework completion

25 September 2026. Completed the 31 built clusters, M01 through M44, covering all 66 generated body images and their WebP twins. No new merge hub was started.

62 text-card images were rejected and deleted in both formats. Their information is preserved in accessible HTML tables, including existing tables where they already covered it. They were replaced with illustrations that show a mechanism, boundary, hierarchy or decision path. Four existing relationship diagrams were rebuilt at their original filenames. Each preview retains three useful body figures; no gate exemptions were added.

The palette uses #C63600, #FF4500, #FF6F39 and #FF8E64. M01's two brown-cast fields were regenerated and its prompt preambles corrected before the rest of the run. Orange gradients, objects, visible relationships, grain, glow and shadow replace the old white text-card treatment. Labels were composed in Figma after raster generation.
