## business-calling-service-path

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange `#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels, 16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

Type E conceptual service cutaway. Shape: one continuous sweeping connection from a large telephone-number token at left (use a bold hash symbol, no digits), through a carrier antenna in the centre, to a business handset and a small human-headset icon on the right. Behind the handset is a much smaller laptop showing a video-camera icon, clearly a separate application surface, not the same thing as the carrier. The connected route and the separate meeting surface must be visually obvious. Off-white objects and ink route on the orange field, layered paper shapes, readable spatial connections. No labels, words, logo, ratings, boxes, numerical data or purported customer screen. This is a conceptual teaching image, not a diagram of a specific deployed service.
