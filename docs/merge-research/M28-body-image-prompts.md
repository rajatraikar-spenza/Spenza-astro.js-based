## independent-offboarding-confirmations.png

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame
with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange
`#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white
background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a
soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are
confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with
layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and
objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with
blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a
single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat
outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels,
16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

Enterprise offboarding as three independent control circuits feeding a central closure dossier: left a locked phone with a work briefcase icon and a detached key; centre a mobile antenna with its own separate cut-off switch; right a final invoice under a magnifier with a closed ledger. Each has its own distinct confirmation token and separate path to the closure dossier below. No path from phone directly to tower: removing device access does NOT automatically cancel carrier service. Make independent verification visible before text. No actual interface, no prices, no text or numerals. Bright orange full field, slate/off-white objects, grain/glow/shadow. No hills/brown or bordered cards. Reserve top y=20..110 and bottom y=795..900.

Figma labels: Device access, Carrier service, Final invoice, Closure record
