# M14 body-image prompts

These prompts use the four-stop palette. Generated with the image_gen tool; no hero or published asset is an input.

## digital-physical-fulfilment.png

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame
with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange
`#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white
background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a
soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are
confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with
layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and
objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with
blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a
single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat
outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels,
16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled.

A forked fulfilment path from one small mobile-plan checkout kiosk at left. The upper branch is a luminous digital stream carrying one profile tile directly into a compatible phone at upper right. The lower branch is a physical parcel carrying a removable SIM along a winding delivery path to an older-style handset at lower right. Both routes remain possible; neither is shown as universally superior. Objects, forks and endpoints tell the story before words. Bright four-stop orange field, off-white phones, cool blue-grey parcel instead of brown cardboard, ink shadow sides, fine grain and glow. No text, no labels, no numbers anywhere. Reserve blank lower strip for separate typography. No boxed panels or brown shadows.

Typography stage: "Checkout", "Digital", "Physical" in Figma Inter Bold.
