### Intent verdict and AI-answer analysis

**Inferred intent:** commercial investigation with an informational entry point. Readers need to understand a branded mobile offer, then decide which operational responsibilities and supplier terms fit their business. The observed results favor provider pages and concise explainers. Long-tail results add costs and launch checklists. This is an inference from the search sample, not proof of what Google rewards in a particular market.

**Proposed format:** a buyer's launch guide with a direct definition, responsibility comparison, quote worksheet, launch gates and evidence-based provider fit. Prioritize OEMs, MSPs and software platforms over general startup inspiration.

**Observed AI answers:** not measured. No conclusions about which providers Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity or Gemini cite. The following are editorial risks in source material that an AI answer could repeat, not observed defects in a captured AI answer:

- Treating “white label” as proof of a fixed legal or infrastructure model.
- Equating an enabled account with a fully tested commercial launch.
- Treating a carrier list as automatic per-device network switching.
- Treating a wholesale spread as profit after support, payments and minimum commitments.
- Generalizing an IoT regional rollout result into a voice-MVNO launch promise.

**What it would take to compete for first place, without promising a rank:**

1. Answer the definition quickly, then show exactly which contract and ownership choices change the buyer's work.
2. Give the buyer a usable quote-input worksheet and a provisioning-to-billing acceptance test grounded in documented platform behavior.
3. Publish scoped first-hand evidence, including limitations, and support it with a named author and technical reviewer.
