# M10 new body-image prompts

Inventory first; hero reused unchanged. Two conceptual cutaways, minimal text omitted, captions explain the relationship.

## mobile-pool-shared-capacity

Create a rich editorial illustration for a B2B telecom brand. **Fill the frame with a warm colour field** — a soft gradient built from Spenza orange `#C63600`, `#FF4500`, `#FF6F39`, `#FF8E64` — never a plain white background. Add a fine grain or paper texture across the whole image and a soft radial glow behind the focal subject so it sits forward. Shapes are confident and slightly hand-drawn rather than mechanically precise, with layered depth: a shadow side, a lit side, and a subtle highlight. Icons and objects are simple and bold, in off-white `#FAFAFA` and ink `#282829`, with blue-grey `#7B8898` for secondary detail. Generous negative space around a single clear focal point. No stock-photo people, no clip-art, no flat outlined rectangles, no drop-shadowed bevels, no 3D renders. 1600×900 pixels, 16:9. Any text is large, minimal and correctly spelled. A conceptual paper cutaway of shared data capacity. One off-white reservoir shaped like a broad shallow bowl supplies three unmistakable phone silhouettes through three connected curved channels. One large stream drains toward a phone running a laptop hotspot; the other two narrow streams serve small phones. A clearly visible finite fill line in the shared reservoir makes competition for the same allowance evident. No numbers, labels, tables or fake measurements. Orange gradient whole-frame field, bold ink and off-white paper shapes, grain, soft central glow, shadow sides. Diagrammatic hand-cut collage, not 3D render.

A conceptual cutaway map of business mobile coverage. One large outlined-by-solid-paper-shape orange radio footprint spans a miniature worksite map, with a warehouse cross-section and a field-service van on a road.
